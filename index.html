FAQ

Questions before sending a request?

Do I pay Quick Haul?

Quick Haul is the connection platform. The participating provider handles the actual removal service and final price.

How much does junk removal cost?

It depends on the amount and type of junk, location, access, and the provider. The provider gives the final quote.

Can I send pictures?

Yes. Photos are optional and can help the provider understand the job before contacting you.

Do you guarantee a provider?

No. A request is an inquiry, not a guaranteed booking. Availability depends on participating providers.
